# Canon lock

Part 1 makes these details authoritative. Game content and assets should reference stable IDs rather than inventing names inside scenes.

## Title and ship

- Project title: **Starship Ithaca**
- Vessel: **CSV Ithaca**
- Registry: **CSV-141**
- Class: **Hesperia-class long-range survey cruiser**

Do not use *Wayfarer*, *Indefatigable* or another ship name in Story Mode.

## Player characters

- **Captain Alexander Vale** is the principal player character for beats 1–28 and 30–32.
- **Elara Vale**, his adult daughter, becomes playable in beat 29 and shares the final act.

Do not replace Alexander Vale with Mara Vale. The game may later permit the player to select pronouns or a presentation variant, but that would be a deliberate canon migration rather than ad-hoc scene text.

## Principal entities

- **Tide Gate:** the structure destroyed during the opening battle.
- **Eidolon Host:** the species/civilisation pursuing the Ithaca.
- **Tidefather:** the recurring Eidolon commander and grieving counter-protagonist.
- **Mourning Archive:** the black-hole vault containing uploaded dead.
- **Helios:** a sentient plasma ecology, not merely a star or fuel source.
- **Phaeacian Convoy:** the culture that offers Vale hospitality and judges his account.

## Narrative rules

1. Vale caused the initiating catastrophe, although the intelligence was manipulated.
2. Tidefather’s anger is justified even when the Host’s actions are not.
3. Home changes while the Ithaca is away.
4. The player never receives a universal morality score.
5. Consequences are remembered as specific facts and individual relationships.
6. The sequence of 32 beats is fixed; their human cost and resolution are not.
